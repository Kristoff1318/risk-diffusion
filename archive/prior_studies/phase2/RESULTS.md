# Gaussian sine moments: frozen-control experiment

Completed 2026-10-02 on CPU. No training, downloads, installs, GPU use, guidance integration, or changes to historical event semantics. The original baseline/tag are unchanged. The intervention is opt-in in `revision/sine_moments.py`; `revision/risk.py`, the original sampler, and the validator are unchanged.

## Protocol and mathematical scope

Pre-evaluation commit **e4ac7fc** froze the code, 124 control sequences, task splits, seeds, measurements, and reference budgets. There are 24 tuning and 32 evaluation B0 tasks in each of two dynamics settings (a=.12/.35), plus all 12 preserved nonlinear conventional optimizer plans as diagnostics. Existing diffusion checkpoints generated the B0 controls on CPU once before evaluation. Task seeds 2026100301 and 2026100401 separate tuning/evaluation. No parameter was tuned on either set or on the known counterexample. `PROTOCOL.json` and `FROZEN_CONTROLS.npz` are tracked.

For Gaussian input to one update, the sine means and full sine/sine and state/sine cross-covariances are analytic. Independent 31x31 Gauss–Hermite quadrature checks positive and negative input cross-covariances at <5e-16 absolute error. Affine, noise-zero, gradient finite-difference, unchanged risk-event formula, and PSD/finiteness checks pass. The random multistep check and every evaluated case stay PSD; no eigenvalue projection was applied.

**Exact one-step Gaussian-input moments do not make the next nonlinear state Gaussian.** The recursively reapplied Gaussian closure is approximate after the first nonlinear update. Circle-to-tangent and Gaussian-tail approximations, and whole-trajectory union aggregation, remain. This is not an exact chance bound or a certificate.

Both methods share 8,192 full nonlinear reference executions per case for state/marginal comparisons. A separate 32,768-execution audit supplies risk and decision comparisons. Thresholds are .05/.10/.20. A confirmed false-safe prediction requires predicted-safe controls and a fresh audit two-sided 95% lower risk limit above the threshold. These per-case intervals are not simultaneous familywise guarantees across 124 cases. Controls and physical uncertainty are identical between estimates; proposal spread is not execution noise. Obstacles are checked at x0..x[H-1] and the terminal goal at xH, exactly as in the baseline.

In stronger nonlinear dynamics, terminal mean error falls .04035 -> .00255 (94%); terminal covariance error .00358 -> .00268 (25%); cross-covariance MAE .000375 -> .000255 (32%); tangent marginal MAE .001237 -> .000766 (38%). Yet full-trajectory risk MAE changes only .04143 -> .03978 (4%). Worst audit-risk underestimation improves .01083 -> .00558, but remains nonzero. Moderate-noise covariance improvement is small.

Both methods have **zero confirmed and point-estimate false-safe predictions at all three thresholds on these held-out controls**, and the same predicted-safe counts: moderate 25/28/31 and nonlinear 27/27/28 at .05/.10/.20. Only 3/64 held-out controls are confirmed unsafe at .10, so no general false-safe-rate reduction is established. The 48 tuning cases show the same direction of moment improvement and are reported separately in SUMMARY.json.

## Historical boundary diagnostics

All 12 old nonlinear optimizer controls are audit-confirmed unsafe at .10. The Jacobian surrogate calls 11 safe; analytic closure calls none safe. This is a diagnostic correction on a bank generated by the old optimizer, not an independent generalization-rate estimate.

On the known task-3 counterexample:

- Jacobian predicted event sum: **.099403**.
- Analytic closure predicted sum: **.114903**.
- Fresh independent audit: **.114624**, 95% CI **[.111194,.118123]**; validator rejects.
- Terminal mean error against the matched reference: .03716 -> .00053; terminal covariance error .00391 -> .00179.
- The matched reference tangent marginal sum remains .129883. Analytic closure still underestimates that sum by .01498; geometry adds .00781 and event aggregation adds .00562. Agreement with the joint audit risk partly reflects cancellation of these errors, not an exact probability model.

Across the historical diagnostic bank, trajectory risk MAE falls .01335 -> .00272 and worst underestimation .01983 -> .00402. Some closure risks still underestimate audit risk, even though they cross the tested .10 rejection threshold.

## Remaining error and runtime

For the held-out nonlinear bank, the average union excess is .03555, tangent-geometry excess .01328, and closure's Gaussian-minus-empirical-tangent sum is -.01259. Conservative temporal/obstacle aggregation and geometry largely offset distribution approximation errors. State-moment improvement reduces marginal errors, but does not eliminate the non-Gaussian tail approximation or joint-event bookkeeping error. This decomposition changes with controls and must not be treated as a reusable safety buffer.

Average CPU propagation-plus-risk time is about **.55 ms Jacobian versus 1.05 ms analytic closure** per plan, same float64/thread configuration and five repeats. This is a roughly 1.9x local cost, not a measured whole-planner cost. No guidance trajectories were generated with the new energy and no accepted-plan cost or planner-runtime improvement is claimed.

## Decision and next bounded step

The intervention merits **opt-in guidance integration and a matched small test**, because moment/marginal improvement appears on separate tasks and the diagnostic correction is substantial at modest absolute cost. It does not merit replacing the default, reducing validation, or claiming a chance guarantee. Gradients were checked numerically; optimization could still exploit remaining tail/union errors.

Proposed next step (not launched): 16 new evaluation tasks per dynamics regime, existing checkpoint, matched seeds/proposals and 64 denoising/32 correction stages; compare B0, original chance, closure chance, and K=256 CVaR under unchanged 4,096 validation/8,192 audit draws at .05/.10. Fix lambda=8, gamma=30, no new training and no tuning on that audit. Measure rejection rate, cost, fresh accepted-plan risk, gradient stability and full runtime including validation. Preserve a small conventional/final-only refinement comparator if it fits the agreed budget. Estimate 30–120 CPU seconds and <20 MB; use a hard timeout after authorization. No GPU is required for this size. A broader cost sweep or manipulator conversion remains a later separately scoped step.
